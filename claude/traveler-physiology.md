# Traveler Physiology — preparing a human body for time travel, the side effects, and the glitch ledger

What kind of glitches and timeline splits can we expect?" Fourth sibling: see claude/greylock-time-machine.md, claude/starship-time-machines.md, claude/time-machine-catalogue.md. ## 0. The headline, in two sentences

Time dilation itself requires no preparation, causes no sensation, and has no biological side effects even in principle — the clock hypothesis says every clock, mitochondria included, runs on local proper time alone, and this is verified experimentally at 10¹⁸ g of proper acceleration (CERN muon storage ring: Bailey et al., Nature 268, 301, 1977). Every real hazard of time travel belongs to the *vehicle*; the one intrinsic side effect of the travel itself is that there is no return ticket.

## 1. Why there is nothing to prepare (the clock hypothesis)

Time dilation is not a force, a field, or a stress. In the traveler's own frame, local physics is exactly ordinary — heart rate, enzyme kinetics, circadian machinery all normal — because dilation is a relationship *between* worldlines, not a condition *on* one. There is no "temporal sickness" for the same reason there is no experiment you can do inside a sealed, unaccelerated box to measure your own absolute speed. The strongest test: muons in the g−2 storage ring, whose decay-clocks are slowed by exactly γ = 29.3 while enduring a proper acceleration of 1.1 × 10¹⁸ g, with no extra effect from the acceleration itself. A human body is a certified relativistic instrument out of the box; Gennady Padalka returned from 878 days in orbit ~22 ms in his own future and required no temporal rehabilitation, only the ordinary astronaut kind.

Corollary worth engraving on the exhibit: **you can never feel yourself time-traveling; you can only compare notes afterward.**

## 2. Preparation by regime (the vehicle sets the prep, not the dilation)

### 2.1 Mountain / mine / tower (nanoseconds)
Prep: none. Greylock's 10 ns/day is ~10⁷× smaller than the daily jitter of the human circadian system. The entire biological protocol is a warm jacket and not dropping the cesium clock.

### 2.2 Orbit (milliseconds)
Everything on the astronaut problem list is microgravity and radiation, not relativity: hip bone-mineral loss ~1–1.5%/month, muscle atrophy, headward fluid shift and the eye-flattening of spaceflight-associated neuro-ocular syndrome (SANS), immune dysregulation, ~0.3 mSv/day aboard the ISS (~100× ground). Prep is the existing countermeasure stack: resistive exercise ~2 h/day, bisphosphonate protocols, vision monitoring.

The NASA Twins Study (Garrett-Bakelman et al., Science 364, eaau8650, 2019) is the twin paradox run with actual twins, and its verdict is the whole section in one line: Scott Kelly banked ~9 ms of relativistic youth over 340 days (−25 µs/day) and came back *biologically more aged than his twin* — radiation and microgravity outspent relativity by many orders of magnitude. The in-flight telomere lengthening (and post-flight crash) that pop articles attribute to time dilation is stress biology; gene-expression changes were ~90% back to baseline within six months. Current scorecard of human forward time travel: dilation banked, milliseconds; biological cost, measurable; biological effect *of the dilation*, zero.

### 2.3 Torchship (years → megayears; the 1g starship of the sibling note)
Here the physics turns protective and the engineering turns lethal:

- **1g acceleration is a health feature.** The torchship cures microgravity by design — the same 9.8 m/s² that is the relativity engine is the gravity the body evolved for. No bone protocol needed.
- **The interstellar medium becomes a particle beam.** At 0.2c, ~1 H atom/cm³ arrives as ~6 × 10⁹ protons/cm²/s at ~19 MeV ((γ−1)mc², γ = 1.0206) — a proton-therapy beam aimed at the whole ship, stoppable by modest forward shielding (19 MeV protons stop in millimeters of metal, centimeters of water). By γ ≈ 2 each atom is a ~GeV cosmic ray and the forward shield is meters of ice or active magnetic deflection. Dose scales with nv(γ−1): slow ships are safe, fast ships are reactors.
- **Dust is ordnance.** A single 100 µg interstellar grain at 0.5c delivers (γ−1)mc² = 1.4 × 10⁹ J ≈ 0.33 tons TNT. Whipple shielding, forward sacrificial mass, and route selection through low-dust columns are crew-survival items.
- **The remaining prep is psychiatric and societal**: decades in a closed habitat, and (next section) the arrival problem.

### 2.4 Black-hole orbit (the deep-future parking garage)
Two rules from the sibling note stand: orbit, don't hover; and bigger is safer (horizon tides scale as 1/M², so Sgr A*'s horizon-adjacent tides are gentler than Earth's own gravity gradient). Biologically, the dilation is again imperceptible — and there is a clean no-free-lunch theorem hiding here: **anywhere the proper-time-rate gradient across a 2 m body is large enough to matter biologically, the tidal acceleration across the same 2 m is already lethal.** Gravity always kills you before you can feel time flowing differently through your knees. The actual hazards are the accretion environment (X-rays, relativistic jets — choose a quiescent hole) and the fact that everyone you ever knew is gone when you spiral out.

For calibration: every standing human is already a dilation-gradient system. At 1.09 × 10⁻¹⁶ per meter, an 80-year life leaves your feet roughly **0.5 µs younger than your head**.

### 2.5 The C-category: torpor, the only real "body prep" research program
Where sections 2.1–2.4 need no biological modification, the subjective-time machines are *nothing but* biological modification:

- **Synthetic torpor**: induced hibernation-like states in non-hibernators — adenosine A1-receptor agonism in rodents; Q-neuron stimulation producing multi-day torpor in mice (Takahashi et al. and Hrvatin et al., Nature 583, 2020); ultrasound to the preoptic hypothalamus inducing torpor noninvasively (Yang et al., Nature Metabolism, 2023); SpaceWorks' NASA NIAC crew-torpor studies targeting ~weeks of 10 °C-ish metabolic suppression for Mars transits. Side-effect ledger borrowed from ICU therapeutic hypothermia: arrhythmia, infection susceptibility, coagulopathy, insulin resistance.
- **Cryonics, honestly labeled**: vitrification genuinely prevents ice damage, but cryoprotectants are toxic at loading concentrations and no mammal has returned from liquid-nitrogen storage. What has returned: nematodes (46,000 years, Siberian permafrost — Shatilovich et al., PLOS Genetics 2023), rotifers (24,000 yr), Silene seeds grown after 32,000 yr, tardigrades (30 yr frozen). The honest statement: cryptobiosis is a solved problem at the milligram scale and an open problem at the human scale.
- Taxonomy discipline from the catalogue: all of this is category C — experienced time skipped, proper time wholly unaffected. A torpor pod is a fast-forward button for the passenger, not for physics.

## 3. Side effects of the travel itself

1. **The one-way ticket.** Forward travel's only intrinsic side effect is social: you arrive in a future where everyone else aged. At torchship doses this is bereavement-by-itinerary, and crew selection for a 28-year ride to Andromeda is selection for people who can lose their entire civilization en route. This is a design input, not a footnote.
2. **Dual age.** Law and biology diverge: statutes, pensions, and passports count coordinate (birth-certificate) time; cells count proper time. Padalka is legally 22 ms older than he physically is. At ms doses a curiosity; at torchship doses, contract law for relativistic crews is unwritten.
3. **Compound interest** is the most powerful known application of forward time travel, and also its most dangerous side effect when the bank is on the other end of it.
4. **Jet lag is not a relativistic effect** — Hafele–Keating doses are ~100 ns while circadian desynchronosis comes from crossing time *zones*. A dilated traveler carries their circadian rhythm with them in perfect comfort; the clock hypothesis guarantees it.

## 4. Glitches: real, perceptual, and bureaucratic

- **The simultaneity sweep (Andromeda paradox).** "What is happening on Earth right now" is frame-dependent: during a turnaround burn at Andromeda distance, the traveler's surface of simultaneity sweeps across centuries of Earth history in hours of proper time. Nothing physical happens — no signal, no influence — but the traveler's well-formed-sounding question "what year is it at home?" has no observer-independent answer mid-flight. The deepest genuine glitch in forward travel, and it is purely conceptual.
- **The Doppler movie.** Approaching Earth you see its history in fast-forward; receding, in slow motion — and the visual rates are Doppler rates (×√((1+β)/(1−β))), not the γ bookkeeping rates, so the window and the ledger disagree. Both are right.
- **Timestamp skew.** A returning clock disagrees with every synchronized network it left (GPS, NTP, blockchains). At ns–ms doses this is below or near tolerance; a relativistic courier returning with seconds of skew breaks every "simultaneous" protocol it participates in. Distributed-systems engineering is secretly pre-adapted for this (clock-skew tolerance is standard), which is a nice line for the exhibit.

## 5. Timeline splits: the honest ledger

**Forward travel: none, ever.** One universe, one self-consistent history; dilation bends worldlines and never branches them. No observation in a century of relativistic physics — muons, GPS, Galileo 5/6, the Twins Study — has ever required a second timeline.

**Backward travel (were it possible — the no-go ledger of the sibling notes still stands):** "timeline splits" are not an observed phenomenon but one of three rival consistency bookkeepings, which the catalogue's Grandfather-Paradox Referee (one bench, both prescriptions) exists to distinguish:

1. **Novikov self-consistency** (one timeline, no splits): you always already did whatever you did; paradoxical initial conditions simply have no inconsistent solutions. The real glitch is subtler than paradox: in the wormhole billiard-ball analyses (Echeverria, Klinkhammer & Thorne, PRD 44, 1077, 1991; Friedman et al., PRD 42, 1915, 1990) seemingly paradoxical initial conditions turn out to have *infinitely many* self-consistent solutions — the Cauchy problem loses uniqueness. The malfunction mode of a Novikov universe is not "you killed grandpa"; it is "physics stops predicting which consistent history occurs."
2. **Deutsch's prescription** (splits, in the density-matrix sense): the grandfather circuit's fixed point is ρ = I/2 — the paradox dissolves into a 50/50 mixture readable as "you shot him in the other branch," entropy exactly 1 bit. The nearest thing to pop-culture timeline branching that any serious framework offers.
3. **Post-selected CTCs (Lloyd)** (no splits, conspiracies instead): the universe discards paradoxical histories, so the gun always jams — improbable coincidences pile up exactly as needed. P-CTC side effects are banana peels, not branches.

Above all three sits **Hawking's chronology protection** (PRD 46, 603, 1992): vacuum fluctuations pile up on the Cauchy horizon and plausibly detonate any would-be time machine at the instant of first closure. The universe prefers throwing a segfault to running the paradox. And one clarification worth making loudly, since it is the most common conflation in the genre: Everettian many-worlds branching happens (if it happens) continuously, everywhere, from ordinary decoherence — it is not caused by, affected by, or available to time travelers. Time dilation buys you the future; it does not buy you a sibling timeline.

## 6. The pre-flight checklist (what "making the body ready" actually reduces to)

1. Nanosecond doses (Greylock, mines, airliners): no preparation; the clock is the fragile passenger, not you.
2. Millisecond doses (orbit): train for microgravity and radiation — the standard astronaut stack; the relativity rides free.
3. Year-scale doses (torchship): demand 1g (free health), meters of forward shielding and dust defense (survival), and psychological screening for one-way bereavement (the actual hard part).
4. Century-plus subjective skips: fund torpor research; file cryonics under "unsolved at human scale, honestly labeled C."
5. Backward doses: there is no checklist, because there is no ticket — and if chronology protection is right, the machine explodes at the ribbon-cutting.
